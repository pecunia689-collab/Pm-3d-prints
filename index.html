Need help?

Frequently asked questions

Can I order a custom design?

Yes. Share your STL/3MF/OBJ file or describe your idea, preferred size, colour and quantity. We'll review it and confirm whether it can be printed.

Can I send a photo for a lithophane frame?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp to discuss your photo, frame style, size and lighting options. Share the photo directly in the chat.

How is the final price calculated?

Price depends on model size, material, print time, complexity, finishing and delivery. Product prices shown are starting estimates until confirmed.

How do I pay?

You can message us on WhatsApp to confirm your order and payment, or use UPI. Please verify the recipient name and amount in your UPI app before approving.
